## 1. Keyword Strategy Principles

1. **Topical Entity Anchoring:** Build topical authority around **Visakhapatnam (Vizag)** as the primary transport node, spanning Airport, Railway, City Sightseeing, Hill Stations (Araku, Lambasingi), and Outstation corridors.
2. **Intent Separation:**
   - **Transactional:** Direct booking or booking price enquiry ("book airport taxi vizag", "vizag to araku cab fare").
   - **Commercial Investigation:** Comparing options, vehicle types, tariffs ("best cab service in vizag", "vizag sightseeing car rental with driver").
   - **Informational / Travel Planning:** Route distances, travel times, itineraries ("distance from vizag to bhogapuram airport", "vizag to araku ghat road travel time").
   - **Local / Near-Me:** Hyper-local pickup intent ("cabs near siripuram vizag", "taxi near vizag railway station").
3. **No Fabricated Search Volumes:** Where search volume cannot be independently verified from Google Keyword Planner or search console data, it is marked as *"Volume not verified — strategic keyword."*
4. **Classification Standards:**
   - `CONFIRMED`: Directly observed on Google SERPs, Google Maps local packs, or verified competitor landing pages.
   - `INFERRED`: Derived from logical traveler search behavior and regional transport corridors.
   - `UNVERIFIED`: Strategic long-tail or conversational query.

## 3. Conversational, Voice-Search & AI Query Universe

Modern search engines and AI answer engines (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini) prioritize websites that directly answer specific conversational traveler questions with authoritative facts.

### 3.1 Airport Transit Questions
1. *"How much does a cab cost from Visakhapatnam to Bhogapuram Airport?"*
   - **Answer Target:** Transparent distance (approx. 40–50 km), expected travel time (45–60 mins via NH-16 expressway), and transparent fare range starting from ₹899–₹1,200 depending on vehicle class (Dzire Sedan vs Ertiga MPV).
2. *"Can I book an airport taxi in Vizag for a late-night or early-morning flight?"*
   - **Answer Target:** Yes, 24/7 round-the-clock availability with flight delay tracking and confirmed driver allocation.
3. *"Is there a cab pickup point at Vizag Airport (VTZ)?"*
   - **Answer Target:** Dedicated meet-and-greet curbside service right outside the arrivals terminal gate.

### 3.2 Araku Valley & Tourist Sightseeing Questions
1. *"What is the best way to travel from Vizag to Araku Valley?"*
   - **Answer Target:** A private chauffeured cab offers the most flexible travel experience, allowing scenic stops at Tyda Nature Camp, Katiki Waterfalls, and Borra Caves, with safe driving along the 30+ hairpin ghat road.
2. *"How long does it take by cab from Vizag to Araku?"*
   - **Answer Target:** Approximately 3.5 to 4 hours (115 km) via the scenic S-Kota and Ananthagiri ghat road.
3. *"Can a sedan like Swift Dzire easily climb the Araku ghat road?"*
   - **Answer Target:** Yes, commercially licensed sedans with experienced local drivers navigate the ghat comfortably, while larger groups of 5–7 passengers are best suited for an Ertiga or Innova Crysta.
4. *"What is included in a 1-day Vizag city sightseeing cab tour?"*
   - **Answer Target:** 8 Hours / 80 KM covering Kailasagiri Hilltop, INS Kursura Submarine Museum, TU-142 Aircraft Museum, RK Beach, and Rushikonda Beach with doorstep pickup and drop.

### 3.3 Intercity & Billing Policy Questions
1. *"Do I have to pay return fare for a one-way cab from Vizag?"*
   - **Answer Target:** No, Waltair Cabs provides genuine one-way billing with zero return kilometer charges on designated routes like Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Rajahmundry, and Vijayawada.
2. *"How early should I book an outstation cab in Vizag?"*
   - **Answer Target:** Advance booking 2–4 hours prior is recommended, with instant booking dispatch available for urgent departures.
